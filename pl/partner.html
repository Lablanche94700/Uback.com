Become Uback’s exclusive partner in Poland

Uback ranks a country’s startups and aggregates the investment intentions of business angels, family offices and diasporas, in Europe, the Gulf and elsewhere. A single partner per country executes: you.

What Uback brings you

  • Investors you would not find on your own. The intentions declared on your market are reserved for you: amounts, sectors, sought-after companies, with the identity of the Backers who agreed to be introduced.
  • Visibility. Your name, regulatory status and registration number appear on every ranking of the country (“Introductions made by…”), and on your partner page.
  • A dashboard. Alerts when intentions on a company or a sector cross the threshold, deal tracking, a record of every contact.
  • A quarterly prospecting kit. A summary of the intentions on your market, to send to your own clients.
  • A voice. You can publish notes under your name on your market.

What Uback does not bring you

A guaranteed deal flow. In a market like Poland, with around 180 venture rounds a year across all players, Uback is an additional channel for investors and visibility, not a source of immediate revenue. We would rather say so upfront.

The framework

  • Exclusive per country, annual contract, renewable and renegotiable according to the audience.
  • You alone remain in charge of regulated activities: contacting companies, mandates, advice, structuring, negotiation, collection of funds. Uback does none of this.
  • Remuneration: an annual fee, as an advance on the retrocessions due on deals closed with Backers introduced by Uback.
  • Profile sought: investment firm licensed by the KNF, investment bank or M&A boutique, with experience of private companies and of English.

For managers: the Challengers

An eligible company that is not in the ranking will be able, for a fee, to appear in a separate list labelled “Challengers”, for a set period, with a memo available to verified Backers. The listing has no effect on the ranking. Opens once the partner signs.
